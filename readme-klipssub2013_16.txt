Replication data for "Efficient Estimation of Linear Panel Data Models with Correlated Random Effects and Sample Selection" by Chirok Han and Goeun Lee (2026)

The data "klipssub2013_16.dta" (for Stata) was extracted from the Korean Labor and Income Panel Study (KLIPS) data. It consists of a balanced panel of 1,241 married women aged 25 to 45 in 2013, observed annually from 2013 to 2016. Variable names and descriptions are provided below.

Observations: 4,964

01. id
02. year
03. wave: wave from 16 to 19
04. inlf: =1 if in labor force
05. hours: hours worked (per year)
06. kidslt6: # kids < 6 years
07. kidsge6: # kids 6-19 years
08. age: age in years
09. age13: age in year 2013
10. educ: years of schooling
11. wage: estimated wage (per hour)
12. faminc: family income (per year)
13. exper: experience in years
14. expersq: exper^2/100
15. nwifeinc: (faminc-wage*hours)/1000
16. lwage: log(wage)
